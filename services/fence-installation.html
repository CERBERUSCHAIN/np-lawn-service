Fence Installation & Repair Questions

How long does a fence install take?
A typical residential privacy fence takes 1–3 days depending on length and terrain.
Cedar or pine?
Cedar lasts longer and resists rot and insects naturally; pressure-treated pine costs less. We will price both.
Do I need a permit?
Most Denton County cities require one for new fences. We will tell you what your city needs.
