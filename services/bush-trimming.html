Bush Trimming Questions

How often do bushes need trimming?
Most North Texas shrubs look their best with 2–3 trims a year. Fast growers like ligustrum may need more.
Can you bring back badly overgrown shrubs?
Usually yes. Some species tolerate a hard cutback in late winter; others need it staged over two seasons. Noel will tell you which.
Do you trim around utilities and AC units?
Yes, and we keep proper clearance around your AC unit so it runs efficiently.
