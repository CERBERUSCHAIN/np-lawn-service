Bed Edging Questions

How often does edging need to be redone?
Cut-in edges hold 4–8 weeks. On recurring service we touch them up as needed.
Is permanent edging worth it?
If you hate the maintenance, yes. Steel edging is nearly invisible; stone edging adds a decorative border.
Can you edge around trees?
Yes — clean tree rings are one of the fastest ways to upgrade a front yard.
