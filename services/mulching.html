Mulching Questions

How much mulch do I need?
Roughly one cubic yard covers 100 sq ft at 3 inches. Noel measures your beds and orders the right amount.
Which mulch color is best?
Natural hardwood is the classic choice for North Texas homes; black mulch gives a modern look. Dyed mulches fade slower than you would think with today's products.
How often should mulch be replaced?
A full refresh every spring, with a light top-off in fall if needed.
