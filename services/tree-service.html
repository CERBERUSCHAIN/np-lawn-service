Tree Service Questions

When is the best time to trim trees in Texas?
Late winter (Feb–March) for most species, before spring growth. Oaks especially should avoid Feb–June to prevent oak wilt. We schedule accordingly.
Do you remove large trees?
We handle small and medium trees. For very large removals or anything near power lines we will tell you honestly and point you to a licensed arborist.
Is the debris included?
Yes. Trimming, cleanup, and haul-off are all in the quote. No surprises.
