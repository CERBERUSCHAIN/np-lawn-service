Stone Work Questions

Is stone cheaper than mulch long term?
Yes. Higher upfront, but you never replace it. Most rock beds pay for themselves in 3–4 years of skipped mulch.
Do you do large retaining walls?
We handle small decorative walls (under 2 ft). Structural walls need an engineered design — we will tell you honestly.
Can you convert an existing mulch bed to rock?
That is one of our most common jobs. We clear, fabric, edge, and fill.
