Flower Beds Questions

What flowers do best in North Texas?
Lantana, salvia, vinca, and pentas for summer; pansies and snapdragons for cool months. Perennials like knockout roses and dwarf yaupon give year-round structure.
Can you redo an existing bed?
Absolutely. We clear it, fix the soil, re-edge, replant, and re-mulch.
How much does a new flower bed cost?
It depends on size and plant choices. Noel will walk the space with you and give a firm number before any work starts.
