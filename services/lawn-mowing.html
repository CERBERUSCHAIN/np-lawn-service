Lawn Mowing Questions

How often should I mow in Texas?
Weekly during peak growth (April–September), bi-weekly in spring and fall, and as-needed through winter. We adjust with your lawn.
Do you bag the clippings?
Either. Mulching returns nutrients to the soil; bagging keeps things pristine. Your call, same price.
What size yards do you handle?
Standard residential lots start at $35. Larger properties, acreage, and commercial are quoted after a quick look.
